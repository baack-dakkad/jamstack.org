## Baack

Baack is a pro-code API first digital experience platform with content at it's core.

* Fully featured content model.
* Versioning, language and personalisation out of the box.
* Build sophisticated publication and customer engagement experiences around content using projects, tasks, communities, chat and more.

### Pro-code and API-first

Every experience offered from Baack is built API first with tools to help you create in-site or in app editorial workflows.

We also have a rich admin console as a backup but our platform is designed to let content creators write where the content lives in the app or site.

### Rich content model

Our content API supports all standard content concepts from raw text, markdown, to full template support. Our platform is designed for COPE omni-channel delivery.

### Start small and scale

Our platform is designed to deliver your content to any device. On-premisis deployment is available to enterprise customers. We offer a generous free tier for qualifying startups an charities.
